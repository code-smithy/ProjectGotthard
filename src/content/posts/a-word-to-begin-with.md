I started this project a couple of months ago with a friend. No idea where we're heading.

## Standing at the foot of the giant

I'd say "Gotthard" is a pretty ample metaphor for this project.

Both of us are fans of the beloved sci-fi series "Stargate SG-1" and for some reason we got talking about turning the setting
into a video game. Somehow (bar one exception), the Stargate series never received one (the movie did, but in the 90s) and
we kept wondering why. And how one would look like. 

With my background in software engineering we got cracking. We gathered ideas and compiled something that resembled a design document.
It shaped up to be a management game, basically the series from General Hammond's point of view at the SGC. That can't be hard to do, right? Right!?

## What did you do!? (he said calmly)

And now, after a couple of weeks I have a prototype with several different mechanics, as envisioned. Obviously there are teams,
missions, planets and exploration. Some of the features (That actually already work):

- Recruit personnel and assemble exploration teams.
- Equip and prepare teams before missions.
- Choose destinations and manage the exploration programme.
- Evaluate reports, intelligence, and scientific discoveries.
- Make diplomatic and strategic decisions.
- Respond when missions go wrong.

Well, I say work. They are functional, there are mechanics and simulations, but require a lot of love and touching up. 
Right now, it mainly consists of boring text and abstract concepts. So we don't really have anything
that could be called a "GUI". Right now, it's more like "Goo-ey" (aka. AI-Slop). The hard part is bringing it all together and making it an enjoyable 
experience for the player. Because... art is hard and I want it to feel alive! This is why I cannot do this alone and I want to hire somebody in the long run.

## What are you trying to tell me?

What do I want with this?

Since this is my first project of this calibre, I wanted to document the process. For myself, as well as to connect with 
people that would potentially want to play something niche like this. I'll go into the full vision in a later blog post,
I guess I'll share parts of the design document and vision, as well as some screenshots from the game itself. I am aware
that this only speaks to a very small group of people. And I hope you are one of them.
