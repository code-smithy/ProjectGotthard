The public setting of Project Gotthard starts in an underground Alpine facility in an alternate 1994. That gives the programme a physical home before it takes anyone beyond Earth.

## A grounded starting point

The intended atmosphere draws on Alpine military engineering, restrained science fiction and the material character of the early 1990s: workspaces, documents, displays and the people who use them.

The aim is a place that feels purposeful. Exploration is extraordinary; the responsibility of running a programme should feel human and concrete.

## A visual language for the journal

The Gotthard Files uses paper tones, dark blue panels, fine rules and survey-like graphics. These are website design choices. The Alpine schematic on this site is an original decorative illustration, not a game screenshot or evidence of a completed game location.

## Showing work honestly

No gameplay images accompany this first note. When development captures are ready for publication, they will identify what is shown and whether it is current gameplay, work in progress or a concept.

Further reports will connect visual choices to the command experience as there is approved work to discuss.
